ABOUT US

Ashoka Women's Engineering College

Founded in 2008, the college is an exclusive residential campus for women, set on 10.6 acres in Kurnool, Andhra Pradesh, making it one of the larger colleges in the district. It offers B.Tech, M.Tech, MBA and MCA programs.
